Failure categories — cards vs UPI

The two rails fail for structurally different reasons, which is why payment_method is a real feature rather than decoration. Cards skew to issuer-side hard declines; UPI skews to operational drop-off and soft declines.

card UPI

What the agent did

Thresholds were derived from the model's measured score distribution, not guessed. Fraud-blocked transactions are never actioned and never scored — an architectural rule, not a learned preference.

Retry storm — before / after

The retry loop was deliberately built without a cap first. Every polling cycle re-queued anything that hadn't succeeded, so the storm concentrated on transactions that could never succeed. Fixed with a per-transaction budget and exponential backoff.
