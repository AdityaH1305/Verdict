Why the payments failed

Cards and UPI fail for different reasons, so Verdict treats them as two different problems rather than one failure rate.

Card UPI

What we did about it

Each payment gets the response its failure actually calls for. A bank timeout wants another attempt; a customer who walked away wants a reminder; a permanent decline wants to be left alone.

We stopped hammering the banks

The first version of the retry loop kept trying until something worked. Mostly it retried payments that could never work — which costs you goodwill with your bank and your customer, and recovers nothing.

How this works

Two models, then a decision

One model works out why a payment failed. A second estimates whether a recovery attempt would actually succeed. Only then does anything get decided.

payment failed | v why did it fail? ----> blocked as fraud | | | stop here v would recovery work? | v what should we do? ----> and why we did it

Fraud is stopped, not scored

A payment diagnosed as fraud never reaches the recovery model and never reaches a language model. It is not a filter applied afterwards — there is no path through the code where it gets that far.
